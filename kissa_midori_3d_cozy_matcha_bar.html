02 / THE VIENNOISERIE

Laminated Butter & Ceremonial Glazes

Every morning, our illuminated case fills with viennoiserie tailored specifically to pair with the rich umami and gentle astringency of single-cultivar matcha. Fluted Bordeaux canelés with crisp matcha crowns, flaky croissants, and burnt Basque cheesecake.

03 / EXTRACTION & MILLING

The Granite Ishiusu Mill

Our grooved Uji Mikage granite stones rotate at exactly 60 revolutions per hour. This ultra-slow cold-grinding prevents friction heat from degrading volatile chlorophyll and amino acids, creating vibrant green tencha micro-powder with remarkable sweetness.

04 / THE RITUAL

Hand-Whisked Ceremonial Usucha

Prepared fresh to order in matte-black Kuro Raku chawan bowls using an 80-tine Takayama bamboo chasen. Whipped in rapid W-motions to create a dense jade micro-foam with velvety texture and lingering umami.

05 / THE SIGNATURE

Layered Iced Kuro Matcha Latte

A three-tier study in density and temperature served at our walnut window counter. Choose between our signature espresso-cut Dirty Matcha, creamy oat foam, or first-harvest pure usucha over crystalline ice.
